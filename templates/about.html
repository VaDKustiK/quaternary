{{ _('The Quaternary Period') }}

{{ _( 'The Quaternary period, or Anthropogene (Quaternary system; ' 'Anthropogenic period; Quaternary) is a geological period ' '(geological system), the modern stage of the Earth\'s history, ' 'the third (current) period of the Cenozoic era. It began 2.58 ' 'million years ago and continues to this day.' ) }}

{{ _( 'This is the shortest geological period, but it was in the ' 'Quaternary that most modern landforms were formed in their ' 'modern form and many significant events in the history of the ' 'Earth (from the point of view of man) occurred, the most ' 'important of which are the glaciation periods and the emergence ' 'of man. In such a short time interval, radiocarbon analysis ' 'and other methods based on the decay of short-lived isotopes ' 'are used primarily to determine age. The specificity of the ' 'Quaternary period compared to other geological periods created ' 'a special branch of geology – Quaternary.' ) }}
